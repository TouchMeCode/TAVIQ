# TAVIQ Name and Attribution Policy

## The TAVIQ name

TAVIQ is the original name and identity of the programming language project created by
[TouchMeCode](https://github.com/TouchMeCode) and maintained at
<https://github.com/TouchMeCode/TAVIQ>.

TAVIQ is **not** a registered trademark at the time of writing. This policy describes how the
project asks others to use its name. It does not claim legal rights beyond those that may exist
under applicable law.

## Code license and name are separate

- The source code is licensed under MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- Neither license grants permission to use the project's name, logo, or other identifying marks
  in a way that suggests official status. Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 states this
  explicitly. The MIT License does not mention trademarks.
- Copyright, license permissions, and name or trademark rights are three different things.
  Permission to copy the code is not permission to represent yourself as the TAVIQ project.

## Attribution policy

### A. The original TAVIQ project

The original and official TAVIQ project is the repository at
<https://github.com/TouchMeCode/TAVIQ>, maintained by its original creator and the maintainers
the creator appoints. Official releases are published only from that repository.

### B. Independent forks

- Forking the repository is permitted under the license.
- A fork is an independent project. It does not become the official TAVIQ project.
- A fork should say clearly that it is a fork of TAVIQ and link to the official repository.

### C. Modified distributions

- Modified versions must keep the copyright and license notices required by the license they are
  distributed under.
- A modified distribution should not be called "TAVIQ" alone. Use a different name, or a clearly
  descriptive form such as "ExampleLang, based on TAVIQ".
- The licenses do not require modified versions to publish their source code, and this policy does
  not add that requirement.

### D. Third-party extensions and integrations

- Tools, libraries, editor plugins, and integrations may say that they work with TAVIQ, for
  example "a syntax highlighter for TAVIQ".
- They should not use names or descriptions that suggest they are made or endorsed by the official
  project, unless the maintainers have agreed to this.

## What is not allowed

- Claiming to be the original creator of TAVIQ when you are not.
- Claiming authorship of the original project or of other people's contributions.
- Stating or implying official affiliation, endorsement, or partnership without permission from
  the project maintainers.
- Presenting a fork or modified version as the official TAVIQ project.

## What is welcome

- Describing your own contributions accurately, for example "I contributed the parser error
  messages to TAVIQ".
- Writing articles, tutorials, and talks about TAVIQ.
- Linking to the official repository.

## Logo

TAVIQ does not have an official logo yet. When one is published, its usage guidelines will be
added to [docs/BRAND_PROTECTION.md](docs/BRAND_PROTECTION.md).
